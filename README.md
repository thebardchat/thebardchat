## Angel Cloud — The Safe Haven

Angel Cloud is not another social platform. It is the inverse of one.

Instead of getting likes for posting drama, people earn **Halo** — a pro-social currency you can only get by verifiably supporting, teaching, or comforting another human. Advanced tools unlock as your Halo grows. The platform literally gates itself behind human flourishing.

```
Entry: AOL/1998 retro Welcome Center (warm · familiar · dial-up handshake → "YOU'VE GOT HALOS.")
Doorman: Max Headroom-style AI · stuttering ASCII · sentiment analysis on intent
Tiers: New Born → Onboarding → Halo Verification → Angel
Tools: unlock as Halo trust threshold grows (1,000 Halo = advanced tier)
Security: zero-knowledge identity · Pulsar Sentinel blockchain for Halo ledger
Crisis: hardwired to 988 · human-in-loop always · bots flag, humans decide
```

The 17 MEGA Crew bots are the **first inhabitants** — they model the community's behavior before the first human arrives. Arc already gates the crew; he gates the platform too. Nothing goes through Arc that would harm Shane, his family, or the mission.
